Research

Learning robot skills that survive contact, drift, and hardware reality.

01

Humanoid Motion Skills

Reinforcement learning methods for complex humanoid skills such as walking, dancing, and backflips, with curriculum assistance that fades as the policy becomes competent.

02

Loco-Manipulation

Hierarchical policies that reason in world coordinates for long-horizon end-effector tracking while maintaining dynamic stability under locomotion and contact.

03

Legged Robot Control

Controllers for quadruped and multi-legged robots, including quiet indoor locomotion and morphology-agnostic policy distillation.
